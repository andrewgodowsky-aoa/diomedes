## Position and sequence

**Your hardware. Your data. Your AI.** Diomedes can become the operating layer for AI a business owns without requiring that business to become an infrastructure company. This is engineering, integration, reliable business workflows and support. Hardware procurement is not a profit center.

Keep one configurable product across industries. Customer-owned infrastructure is particularly relevant to larger organizations, multi-location operators and restaurant groups with sustained useful work. It is an expansion path, not a replacement for the first-customer plan:

1. Internal/personal use.
2. Consultant operating layer.
3. Narrow design-partner restaurant workflow pilot.
4. Measured case study, published only with permission.
5. Repeatable hospitality offering.

After workload evidence exists, the customer can expand from workflow pilot to implementation, capability pack, private/hybrid infrastructure and optional Managed Diomedes. These are possible next scopes, not mandatory purchases. The first restaurant still proves a bounded workflow with authorized inputs; hardware is not a day-one dependency. Starter and its three-month terms are governed by the pricing authority (the monthly figure that stood here was retired and removed on 2026-09-19).

## What a deployment buys

A scoped engagement can include workload assessment; cloud/local/hybrid economics; architecture and sizing; acquisition guidance; model/runtime selection; installation; security configuration; Diomedes setup; connectors and capability packs; knowledge/data integration; permissions and approvals; benchmarking; remote management where implemented and authorized; training; documentation; handover; support and optimization.

Example assessed workloads for a restaurant group: management reports, SOP retrieval, invoice/vendor analysis, review aggregation, marketing drafts, classification/extraction, monitoring, recurring jobs, internal staff questions and authorized system integrations. These are candidate capabilities, not a claim of supported production connectors. Begin with files/exports when sufficient. Packs compose the existing Runtime, Trust, Project, tools, Agents and context; activation grants no authority.

## Deployment choices

| Mode | Why it may fit | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloud | Light demand, frontier reasoning, weak local economics | Approved data destination, account, payer and spending policy |
| Private / local | Sustained volume, ownership, privacy/data residency, predictable capacity | Local quality, concurrency and operating costs must fit actual tasks |
| Hybrid | Routine/private/high-volume work locally; more demanding reasoning on approved cloud routes | Explicit customer policy for each data class and destination; no silent cloud fallback |

Hybrid will often be the assessment hypothesis for substantial deployments, not an automatic setting. A local outage must queue/refuse restricted work rather than export it. Remote customer-owned inference is still a network/data boundary even when it is inside the same organization.

## Equipment and pricing

Use workload-sized, evidence-backed categories rather than fixed SKUs: compact local node, performance GPU node, high-memory unified-memory node, multi-GPU node and enterprise/custom deployment. None is currently validated or a hardware recommendation.

Size from workload/model requirements, simultaneous users/jobs, context, latency, uptime, growth, privacy, budget, power, noise and environment. Support dense and MoE models, multiple specialized models and combinations of small/large models, embeddings, reranking, vision and speech; allow quantizations, CPU-assisted inference, GPU, unified memory and future multi-machine deployments. A parameter count or advertised memory capacity is not an acceptance test.

## Ownership and support exit

Ending Managed Diomedes must not intentionally make purchased local infrastructure useless. Retain a functional self-managed deployment to the extent technology and licensing permit. Implementation handover must make this concrete:

- Customer-owned administrative access and an inventory of components, licenses, versions and configuration.
- Documented start/stop, local operation, backups, restore and recovery; tested customer-run handover.
- Ordinary export of customer data and configuration, with credentials transferred/rotated through the existing secure authority path rather than copied into documents.
- An explicit list of optional managed functions and external paid services that end, change responsibility or incur costs after support cancellation.
- Revocation of Diomedes support access without disabling customer-owned local operation or destroying records. Paid company-funded inference still requires entitlement; local/BYO must not silently consume it.

Managed fees buy monitoring, health alerts, model/runtime/security upgrades, connector maintenance, capability-pack updates, troubleshooting, optimization, additional agreed workflow work, remote support and ongoing tuning. Cancellation does not promise perpetual free labor, third-party services or license rights Diomedes cannot grant. Existing published software rights are not amended here.

## Honest economic assessment

Future tool: **Should Your Business Run AI Locally?** Compare cloud, local and hybrid on the same workload over three and five years. Inputs: users/locations, subscriptions, API usage, recurring automated volume, privacy, availability, equipment, implementation, power, support, expected life, residual value/replacement and retained cloud usage.

Cloud TCO = setup + subscriptions/API + support over the horizon. Local TCO = equipment + implementation + electricity + maintenance/support + replacements - stated residual value. Hybrid includes local costs plus the cloud costs that remain. Show assumptions, uncertainty and workload quality/latency constraints beside financial results. Do not count the same support twice or invent a linear throughput estimate from model size.

Break-even is the first period cumulative comparable local/hybrid costs fall below cloud, if any; a nonpositive monthly difference or an uncompetitive quality result may produce no break-even. Include sensitivity to utilization and hardware life. Separate privacy, ownership, residency and local availability benefits from financial savings. Replacing one $200/month chatbot alone is not the primary justification.

The assessment must be able to conclude: **Cloud AI is the better choice for your business right now.** Connect "When You Don't Need Local AI" to "What We Don't Automate": no autonomous payroll/payments, hiring/firing/discipline, unapproved orders or unapproved public posts in the initial offering.

## Verified Local and operating commitments

**Diomedes Verified Local** is a future repeatable workload-level evidence program, not an existing certification. Publish exact model/version/artifact, runtime/version, hardware/driver, quantization, context tested, prompt processing and generation rate, concurrent users/jobs, memory, power where measurable, stability duration, workflow tests, failures and limitations. Include unsuccessful configurations and raw methodology; no single synthetic score substitutes for business-task acceptance.

Operating policy can be always-on, business-hours, manual, maintenance-window or supported sleep/wake/service start/stop. An always-on machine with low-power idle may be preferable to nightly shutdown. Do not equate continuous machine operation with 24/7 human support, high availability, offline business connectors or recovery guarantees.
