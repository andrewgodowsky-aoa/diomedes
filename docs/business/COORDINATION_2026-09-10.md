## Disposition of the new consolidation

Keep: practical email/calendar/export/photo inputs, shared capability manifests and workflow packs, controlled distribution/updater work, server-held API credentials, metering, ownership continuity, export/offboarding and cross-industry proof.

Do not silently adopt: a per-location conversion of the $99 organization plan, $29/$39 tiers, mandatory extra setup charges, forced Managed retainers, annual-only setup waivers, unlimited workflows, lifetime 30% discounts, or higher replacement service prices. This reconciliation's 2026-09-10.3 retains approved prices; it is NOT adoption of the separate Fable document called pricing v3.

Do not count the founder's employer or a warm introduction as owned restaurants, a signed design partner, consent to production data or no sales cycle. Do not use the consolidation's uncounted local market size or 1% penetration arithmetic as an acquisition forecast. Location counts and paying-organization counts are not interchangeable.

A shared organization license, named profiles and an owner PIN do not by themselves establish individually authenticated human identity or strong tenant separation. Offline license behavior and server-checked paid access need separate threat models. An unreadable provider legal page does not establish prohibition, permission or an automatic requirement for a special permission letter; assess the actual applicable terms. An API provider switch also needs capability/privacy/evaluation compatibility, not just a new environment variable.

Email/report ingestion does not make every source application's write actions available. Missing information still requires an actual supplied source. A proposed change-order workflow cannot promise 100% capture of unreported field changes. Customer approval, measured outcomes and safe failure behavior remain necessary.

The consolidation proposes $30k-$70k and $80k-$140k first-year revenue, and $150k-$300k in year two. Those rely on unapproved higher/per-location prices, assumed acquisition and delivery timing. They are scenarios, not a corrected empirical forecast. Use the explicit approved-price customer-month model for current planning; higher paid project values can be tested through accepted custom quotes.
