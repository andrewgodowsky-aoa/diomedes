The public entry offer is the 90-Day Workflow Starter: $250/month for 3 months, $750 total, with no extra Business subscription. It covers one supported workflow and remote setup; custom engineering and on-site work are separate. After 90 days, stop or separately agree the next scope. The wider ladder is a free fit call, Local Hardware and Model Plan from $500, Cloud AI Quick Start from $750, audit from $1,000, pilot from $2,500, implementation from $5,000 plus $2,000 for each location after the first, Private AI deployment from $15,000 plus hardware, managed service from $750/month and Fractional AI Ops from $2,000/month. Business software is $300/org/month once operational, including up to 1,000 requests a month and the included design work. Billing, entitlements and operational managed service remain in development.

Local and in-person implementation is a first-class part of the business model when it materially improves discovery, installation, device/network validation, staff training, acceptance testing or go-live support. It is not free travel and is not required when remote delivery is sufficient.

Third-party software changes are an explicit operating risk. Managed Diomedes is partly the paid service layer for compatibility monitoring, smoke tests, configuration drift, triage and repair around named supported systems. It does not guarantee that outside vendors will never break an integration or create unlimited rebuild obligations.
