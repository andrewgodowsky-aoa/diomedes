## Project-memory insertion

Place after Agent, Mode, Team and Project:

### Bot

A Bot is a versioned deployment and recognizable presentation of an Agent for an ongoing role. Its identity persists across model changes. It binds an accountable owner, workspace/organization, authorized Project targets, role/voice, source sets, optional Team, permitted channels, host and policy references. It contains no raw credentials and grants no authority by activation.

Agent describes the worker contract; Model supplies replaceable reasoning; Team composes workers; Automation describes a recurring/event-driven responsibility; Project retains work/context/evidence; Channel carries a conversation. Bot connects those concepts without duplicating their state ownership. Source-event receipt, conversation continuity, task admission and external effect identity remain distinct.

Use current tenant, audience and participant rights at retrieval and egress. Signed incoming content remains untrusted data. Preserve exact provider conversation lineage; portable handoffs contain permitted facts and artifact references, not private reasoning or reusable permission. Keep business knowledge, private continuity, reviewed procedures and candidate lessons separate. Human takeover, ambiguous sends, revocation and downgrade must preserve truthful records and safe resolution.
