A root owns a persisted Team assignment and response; its child has a distinct run identity and no fabricated Session. `HarnessRun.parentRunId` retains fork meaning. Assignment metadata ties it to the root's actual admission and prevents ordinary Team, Work, Loop or Ready paths from starting it independently or completing around approvals.

The lead uses admitted host Team tools, sends the question, waits for persisted mail and consumes a model-produced reply once. The distinct H14 helper reads its sandbox and returns its existing bounded handoff. All role calls use the original ledger object/root job key. Child Runtime units are carved from the root. The existing ledger additionally pins the measured worker's immutable money allocation and checks exposure across connections atomically before provider reservation, including unresolved holds.

Stop closes persisted ownership and cancels only this root's child work. Member Stop reaches its active owned run. Recovery never resends an uncertain dispatch; late results cannot restart a stopped role. Source, membership, profile, account, effort, Trust and privacy changes refuse at the existing admission/dispatch/result/write checks. Paid entitlement checks remain in their ordinary AccountAgentGate path.
