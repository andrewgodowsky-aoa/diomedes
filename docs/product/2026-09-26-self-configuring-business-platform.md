# Self-configuring business platform — SC-2026-09-26.1

**Date:** September 26, 2026. **Status:** Andrew-approved product direction; implementation and release evidence remain separate.

This focused amendment implements Andrew's newest direction in the Core Pillars and supplies the corresponding roadmap and project-memory meanings. It takes precedence over older hospitality-first sequencing and language that defers governed learning to an optional final stage. Preserve all non-conflicting current requirements and newer implementation records. Research baseline: app main `beda88bd49508006cace89571618e47228d38614`.

## 1. Self-service is the destination

A business should increasingly download Nectovia, answer a short adaptive intake, receive a reviewable capability/connection configuration, rehearse useful work and operate without routine founder setup. Paid implementation remains valuable for genuine complexity and optional assistance; it must not be a permanent requirement for basic use.

The proposed advantage is business-specific setup, useful operational workflows, governed organizational knowledge, a broad selectively loaded capability ecosystem and local/BYO/managed execution choices. A feature checklist alone is not differentiation or evidence of customer value.

## 2. Persist configuration under the organization

Save original questions and schema versions, raw answers, structured interpretations and provenance, unresolved fields, configuration candidates, the active configuration revision, pack/dependency versions, source mappings and rehearsal evidence. Business configuration belongs to the organization and is accessed through authorized owner/admin accounts; it is not trapped in the first owner's personal profile.

Reopen, sign-in, another device, invitations and owner transfer load existing configuration. Resume incomplete setup at the correct point. Ask only new or changed relevant questions. Reauthentication, loss of connectivity and an unavailable configuration service are not an unconfigured business. Resolve concurrent edits through expected revisions. A protected local cache cannot revive revoked authority.

Configuration completion, current health, authentication, execution permissions, source authorization and paid entitlement are separate dimensions. Installing a package does not authorize it; activation does not enable a schedule; a rehearsal proves only the exercised inputs and scope. An invited employee joins the existing business rather than rerunning company setup.

Extend the existing `shared/business-setup.ts`, `shared/packs.ts`, account/workspace storage and installed-package contracts after checking their current behavior. Do not create a second questionnaire authority or assume the existing weekly-brief compiler already composes arbitrary capabilities.

## 3. One organizational brain, departmental capabilities

Organization-level policy and shared definitions coexist with department/project capability overlays, scoped connection bindings and role-specific views. HR, finance, IT and management may use different packs without separate runtimes or conflicting company brains.

One brain is a logical coordination and knowledge layer, not a giant shared prompt or a mandatory single physical database. Keep configuration/policy, source systems and rebuildable retrieval indexes distinct. External databases are business data sources, not permission to migrate the existing WorkOS/Neon account control plane.

Source permissions survive retrieval, caches, summaries, embeddings, search results, artifacts, logs, notifications and agent delegation. An organization administrator or supervisory agent does not automatically have authority to read every source. Cross-department work exchanges typed, explicitly authorized inputs and outputs.

Department overlays may specialize tools, procedures, mappings, vocabulary and UI affordances, but cannot weaken organization policy. Active permissions remain the intersection of applicable current grants and scope, not the union of everything any department can access.

## 4. Governed learning is core

Observations and corrections propose private, versioned improvements. Replay and held-out evaluation, authorization, monitoring and rollback govern promotion. Keep sourced facts, preferences, procedural skills, runtime state and enforced policy distinct. Neither repeated success nor a model's confidence changes permissions, data-processing choices or spending authority.

Build the initial learning loop alongside persistent setup and first-value workflows, not after every marketplace and remote feature is finished. An initial slice can turn a corrected mapping or successful bounded procedure into a candidate, evaluate it on retained and held-out cases, present an inspectable diff and reuse an approved revision.

Track derivation from source to summary, mapping, candidate and active skill. Source corrections, revocations and deliberate deletion policies must address affected derivatives. Customer examples and private business logic do not become community packages by default. Automated promotion requires a separately approved bounded policy with independent checks; the proposing model is not its own sole approver.

## 5. Useful authorized data coverage

Evaluate provider-supported APIs, customer-specific access, existing integrations, exports/report feeds, approved email and files, customer-owned database views and permitted attended/browser workflows. Record available fields, missing fields, freshness, supported actions, account/plan/location requirements, cost and proof state.

Do not declare a workflow impossible merely because partner API onboarding is inconvenient. Equally, a connector or MCP wrapper does not create access rights or credentials. Do not bypass authentication, authorization, certification, rate restrictions or data-processing obligations. Missing data stays missing, and an export/read path is not write authority.

The September 26 research corrects the earlier overbroad Toast example: Toast documents customer Standard read-only API access separately from partner/custom routes. Verify the actual customer's eligibility, required objects, locations and permitted integration use. See https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Standard-API-Access and https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/devguide/apiIntegrationTypes.html . No live customer binding was tested in that research.

A source inaccessible through all legitimate routes requires an explicit gap, alternative input, narrower workflow or migration assessment. Do not infer unobserved transactions or claim a full data inventory from partial access.

## 6. Scoped replacement is an eligible service

A paid workflow implementation may retire genuinely redundant software after functional inventory, data migration, operational/security obligations, recovery and exit plans, acceptance testing and total-cost comparison. Missing API access alone is not proof that replacing a system of record is sensible.

Define the exact functions and responsibilities being replaced. Test historical data and attachment access, integrations, reconciliation, permissions, outages, backup/restore, audit/retention, support and cutover. Preserve a usable customer exit. Avoid accidental commitments to rebuild an entire financial, payroll, payment or operational platform through a small workflow quote.

No universal replacement, savings, data portability or new service price is approved by this amendment.

## 7. Cross-industry positioning

Restaurants may provide initial practice and design partners. They are not a mandatory hospitality expansion stage or the center of every example, capability and sales decision. Prioritize cross-industry workflows by usefulness, access readiness, repeatability, risk, willingness to pay and measured support burden. Do not assume every restaurant or every other sector has the same economics.

Services-assisted early deployments are a learning and revenue path toward self-service adoption. Sell recognizable outcomes while retaining the universal configurable core. Preserve the narrow first engagement without making a narrow industry the permanent product identity.

## 8. Broad ecosystem, shared architecture

The approved harness survey is a mechanism library. Capabilities, skills, executable plugins and connection bindings use one governed ecosystem and shared runtime, not copied parallel harnesses. Load relevant modules on demand. Review exact code licenses, versions and compatibility before reuse.

Preserve the 65 original research feature IDs and distinguish additions. A documentation observation is not proof of imported performance, containment, lifecycle parity or production readiness. Reconcile implemented/partial/missing behavior before starting each feature. Use native Nectovia, bounded delegated work and direct external-engine use as explicit execution styles with truthful attribution and controls.

Preserve first-class local/BYO and technical work. A working provider connection alone does not unlock the paid native Agent. A proposed limited setup entitlement, marketplace payments, automated promotion policy and vendor certification remain separate decisions.

## 9. Scale service quality, not employee overload

Measure active retained accounts per delivery/support full-time equivalent together with human setup hours, support and intervention minutes per account, service mix, incidents, retention and accepted-result cost. Productize repeated setup, maintenance and support through reusable capabilities, diagnostics, self-repair and actionable escalation.

No fixed staffing ratio or revenue forecast is approved. More accounts per employee is valuable only when service quality and customer outcomes remain acceptable.

## Delivery and acceptance

Sequence the shared contracts and persistent setup with early source lineage and learning-candidate records; then prove one first-value path. Add departmental authorization, external-source drift and typed handoffs before promising larger-organization coordination. Expand package publishing and execution breadth without requiring every feature before ordinary self-service adoption.

Acceptance must include: restart and second-device resume; invited employee and owner-transfer behavior; concurrent configuration edits; expired authorization without quiz reset; partial/unsupported data; source-schema and meaning drift; cross-department permitted handoff and denied disclosure; tested candidate learning and regression rejection; no permission/payer expansion; and measured setup/support burden.
