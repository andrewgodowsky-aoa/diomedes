# Diomedes Personal and Business — Product contract

Version: PB-2026-09-10.1
Status: Owner-approved product direction; implementation recommendations, not shipped functionality.
Repository home: docs/product/personal-business/

## Product decision

Diomedes Personal gives an individual a capable harness. Diomedes Business gives an organization a configured, maintained AI working environment with bounded managed intelligence.

Build one Diomedes Core and one Console, not divergent forks. A Business instance is a tenant-scoped configuration and entitlement with explicit deployment/data boundaries. It does not automatically mean a dedicated server, a separate executable or ownership transfer of the software.

Personal remains genuinely useful: local/BYO models and supported subscription-native routes, Projects, Agents, Teams foundations, rules, corrections, permissions, evidence, persistence and export. It is self-managed and does not include company-funded inference. Never weaken basic safety, core correctness, readability or recovery to make Business attractive.

Business adds guided configuration; maintained job/industry packs; organization-owned Agents, Teams and knowledge; managed model routing and included usage; organizational policy and approvals; membership; supported connectors; versioned configuration updates; operational visibility; and separately scoped support. This must be a working organizational product, not a cosmetic badge.

## Edition targets

| Area | Personal | Business |
|---|---|---|
| Execution and Trust | Core safety and durable execution | Same Core plus organization administration |
| Models | Local/BYO and supported subscription-native routes | Same options plus entitled managed inference |
| Agents and Teams | User-configured specialists and supported collaboration | Maintained organization jobs, delegation, budgets and approvals |
| Rules and correction | User/project guidance and host policy | Governed organization packs, distribution and reviewed changes |
| Setup | No Business questionnaire | Organization discovery, reviewed configuration and rehearsal |
| Knowledge | Personal/project sources | Tenant/location/project scope and controlled sharing |
| Operations | Self-managed host and local history | Shared work, health/usage visibility and defined management options |
| Local inference | First-class | First-class; no managed API debit for local tokens |
| Exit | Local records and export | Billing lapse does not destroy data or bypass membership rules |

These are target distinctions, not assertions of availability. No unlimited users, locations, hosts, models, support or unattended operation. Industry packs reuse common primitives rather than becoming separate applications.

## Profiles and registration

A person can have a Personal workspace and memberships in multiple organizations. Registration creates or joins a Business organization; it does not permanently convert the whole personal account. The organization owns its Business profile/configuration; the person owns private preferences; membership carries allowed organization-specific preferences and roles. Entitlement and usage balance are separate from identity and runtime permission.

Show Personal and Business: [name] clearly, with active project/location. Bind work, settings, sources, secrets, requests and effects to their correct scope.

The business questionnaire never runs in Personal. An owner/admin starts or resumes company setup. Ordinary invitees join its existing configuration and may answer optional role preferences, not rerun organization discovery. A sole proprietor can buy Business without employees or formal incorporation. Do not claim legal-entity verification without a real process.

## Included Diomedes Agent

Diomedes Agent is the Diomedes-owned supervisory experience, not a claim that proprietary weights exist. Business includes managed access through a commercially appropriate company-controlled API route, alongside organization-permitted local/BYO options.

Preserve human, organization, Agent/revision, Team, model, runtime and payer identities. Direct external-engine work retains truthful attribution. Native Diomedes supervision can use an external model and expose that route in inspection. Personal local/BYO operation remains a genuine Diomedes harness; paying is not what creates intelligence or authority.

## Pricing candidate

Andrew's example is $300 per organization per month including $100 of managed API usage. It is a candidate for validation, not an approved public replacement for planned Business from $99/month or optional Managed from $249/month. Keep services pricing separate.

Recommended accounting: a USD inference allowance consumed by documented eligible upstream charges under a versioned rate card, with no cash redemption or guaranteed task count. Define cost categories, overage, rollover and limits before selling. Retries, reviewers, context and tools can change consumption substantially.

At full $100 usage, $300 minus $100 leaves $200 before fees, infrastructure, support, refunds, taxes and acquisition—not $200 profit. Local/BYO does not debit managed inference allowance; support and orchestration still have costs.

## Self-configuration

Business registration → business-only questionnaire → structured facts → proposed configuration → deterministic validation → permission/connection review → rehearsal → activation → observed operation → reviewed improvement.

The model proposes; the host validates and activates. Answers never authorize spending, sending, payroll changes, deletion, secrets or cross-tenant access. Start with an approved-files/export report or draft. Add synthetic industry variants to prove reuse, not unsupported live-connector claims.

## Immediate delivery

Before GLM's correction run, Opus should establish identity/workspace boundaries, Business intake, versioned setup proposals, Agent/Team/rule integration and honest managed-usage admission. GLM then closes one corrective execution loop using those contracts. Production identity, payments and gateway access have separate readiness gates.

Do not add a marketplace, competing policy engine, universal integrations, foundation-model training or unrestricted remote execution to this pass. The companion documents contain detailed acceptance and future milestones; each short Opus prompt references only its bounded slice.

## Decision status

Approved direction: Personal/Business; capable free Personal; managed Business configuration and Diomedes Agent usage; no Business questionnaire in Personal; Agents/Teams/rules/corrections integration; concise Opus prompts.

Recommendations: separate organization-owned profile, reviewed activation, conservative usage/offline behavior. Commercial candidates: $300/$100 package and exact limits. Implementation is established only by source and actual evidence. See 08_SOURCES_AND_LICENSING.md before treating a desktop entitlement as a license restriction.
