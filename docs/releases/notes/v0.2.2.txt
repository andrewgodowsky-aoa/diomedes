WHAT IS NEW
  A free version beside paid plans, a Usage section with member credit limits,
  paid Automations, a repaired Windows sign-in and routing profiles that keep
  to the limits you accept.

  Nectovia has a free version. Without a paid plan you keep your projects,
  files and workspace, the task board, conversations and work on your own AI
  tools, and your projects' own instruction files. The Nectovia Agent, its
  included usage, Business rules, Automations and phone access come with a
  paid plan. A notice says when you're on the free version, and Settings >
  Account shows your plan.

  Supported conversation engines keep their own sessions across follow-up
  messages, Stops and restarts. When an engine supplies a thinking summary,
  the conversation can show it above the reply.

  Settings has a Usage section. Owners and admins see the business's Agent
  usage this month, the credits it bought and what's reserved for work in
  flight, and a place to buy more. A member sees their own use against their
  own monthly limit. The usage panel warns when 75 and 90 percent of the
  monthly allowance is used or reserved, and again when it runs out.

  Work that runs out of credits stops at that step and keeps what it already
  did. An owner or admin is told to buy more in Settings, Usage, and anyone
  else is told that an owner or admin can buy more.

  A member about to pass their monthly credit limit can ask for that one job
  or for more this month. Owners and admins see the asks under Change
  workspace and approve or decline each one.

  Automations need a paid plan. Without one, their setup and history are kept
  but nothing runs, and a schedule that was on shows Blocked: needs a paid
  plan.

  When a business's paid access ends, the next Nectovia Agent message is
  refused with the reason, the default conversation goes back to your own AI
  tool, and the free version notice returns. When access is granted again, the
  next message picks it up with no restart.

  Personal Agent access and managed usage funding are checked separately. An
  Individual plan covers Personal work and includes monthly credits, and
  Settings > Account shows them with the exact time the period ends.

  A Board task has a Task permission panel: stop at each phase change or
  approve the whole task, cap its action turns and pick an optional playbook.
  Tasks the Nectovia Agent proposes wait in Inbox, and Start on a Board task
  can run on the Nectovia Agent.

  Settings > Account > AI routing and privacy offers two profiles, with
  Balanced selected for a new account. Neither uses your content for training,
  and requests can run outside the US. Balanced prefers providers that don't
  store your content, then US processing, and Lowest cost uses the least
  expensive AI that can do the job. Strict keeps requests in the US with no
  provider storage, and it's available on request at hello@diomedes.net. An
  account that already chose Strict keeps it. Saving names what a looser
  choice gives up, and a choice saved under the earlier rules asks to be saved
  again.

FIXED
  On Windows, signing in no longer stops at a gray screen after you finish in
  the browser. The app now accepts the sign-in callback in the form Windows
  hands it.

  If the account service can't read your plan while you sign in, sign-in still
  finishes. Free work goes ahead, and Nectovia Agent work is refused until
  your access can be confirmed.

  Home lists the newest three items waiting on you, each with Review or Check,
  and its links open the task or approval they name. Needs you counts
  approvals, changes awaiting review and failed work consistently across the
  Console. Review work is described as needing attention rather than requiring
  an OK.

  Account changes and sign-out cannot restore a stale session or borrow an
  admission made for another person. Streamed replies and thinking summaries
  redact secrets split across engine output chunks.

  Marking a task Done by hand is refused while a change waits for review or a
  Need is open.

  Windows briefly locking a record or run file no longer fails the save, a run
  or the weekly brief.

  On a Mac, the red, yellow and green window buttons no longer cover the top
  left of the Console. Search and Stop take Command as well as Ctrl, and
  shortcut hints show the Command key. AI setup no longer links a Mac to a
  Windows installer.

UPDATES
  Settings > App updates offers stable releases only. Nothing is downloaded,
  verified or installed until you confirm it, and a download is accepted only
  when its SHA-256 matches the digest the release publishes. An installed
  0.1.7, 0.1.8, 0.1.9, 0.1.10, 0.1.11, 0.2.0 or 0.2.1 on Windows can find this
  release when you press Check for updates.

  An update from 0.1.9, 0.1.10, 0.1.11, 0.2.0 or 0.2.1 keeps your settings,
  routes, saved keys, projects and conversations. An update from 0.1.7 or
  0.1.8 that skips 0.1.9 still gets the one-time fresh start 0.1.9 introduced:
  the earlier version's data and the Documents\Diomedes folder are set aside,
  renamed with "before 0.1.9" and the date, and nothing is deleted.

  Your install is refreshed the first time this version opens, and again after
  each later update. Settings you never changed move to the new defaults,
  anything you chose is kept, cached screens are cleared, and a byte-for-byte
  backup and a record of what moved are kept. A custom look keeps its colours,
  fonts and artwork, while text sizes, line spacing and density follow the new
  version. You see it once: Updated to 0.2.2, with how many settings moved.

  Saving AI routing and privacy settings needs this version, because the
  account service no longer accepts them from 0.2.1. A choice saved in 0.2.1
  keeps working under its earlier rules until you save it again here.

  An installed 0.2.0 or 0.2.1 on a Mac can find this release when you press
  Check for updates. You then download it from the release page yourself.

LIMITS
  This build is unsigned and experimental. On Windows, SmartScreen may warn
  before the installer runs: choose More info, then Run anyway, after checking
  the file's SHA-256. The Mac build has an ad hoc signature with no Apple
  Developer ID and isn't notarized. The first time you open it, macOS blocks
  it. Then choose Open Anyway in System Settings, Privacy and Security. On
  macOS 14 and earlier, you can also right-click the app and choose Open.

  There's no build for Intel Macs, and the Apple silicon build has been built
  and launched only on one MacBook Air.

  Signing in, Settings > Account and the Nectovia Agent's AI service were
  tested only against stand-ins for the Nectovia account service and its
  sign-in page. The Windows sign-in repair was checked against the exact form
  Windows hands the app, but a real sign-in on an installed copy hasn't been
  run yet. On a Mac, the return to the app after signing in hasn't been tried.
  The account service this version uses is still a staging service with a
  fixed testing budget: while it is, the Nectovia Agent's answers are kept
  short, and the Agent stops answering once that budget is spent.

  Signing in needs the Nectovia account service, so if it can't be reached
  when Nectovia starts, you can't open anything, your own projects included;
  the sign-in screen says why. A connection lost after you sign in doesn't
  sign you out.

  Buying credits isn't available yet. Until payments are set up on the account
  service, Settings, Usage answers that buying isn't available right now, and
  buying has run only against a stand-in payment service.

  Credit limits, bought credits, Individual credits and the paid-access
  changes were tested against stand-ins and test databases, not against the
  live account service.

  This version has no screen for setting a member's monthly limit. Unless one
  is set, a member's limit is the plan's whole allowance.

  When an Individual plan ends, Personal Agent work is refused, but the
  default conversation doesn't move back to your own AI tool the way a
  business's does.

  The new account routing has not yet completed a real signed-in,
  paid-provider acceptance run. Automated routing tests use synthetic model
  responses. A route without current qualification or required privacy consent
  stays unavailable.

  Apart from a few conversation turns on one outside engine and a connection
  check on the company's AI account, nothing in this version was tried against
  a real provider.

  Board work on the Nectovia Agent was tested with stand-in model responses
  only, and helpers and teams don't run on it.

  The phone apps can't reach this computer through the relay yet; this version
  only connects the computer and lists it as online. The relay was tested
  against a stand-in and hasn't yet run on the live account service.

  An update that installs this version over an earlier one, including the
  change from Diomedes.exe to nectovia.exe, hasn't been run on an installed
  copy.

  Automations run the weekly brief alone, and only while this computer is on.
  They tell you inside the app and send nothing by push, email or phone.

  Resume, Retry, Fork and Steer work on ChatGPT (Codex) runs only.

  Run checks run only the checks a task declares, and never run your project's
  own code. Nothing checks a run automatically when it finishes.

  Files has no PDF viewer. Pictures and PDFs cannot be sent to an AI, and a
  binary file cannot be put back from an earlier version.

  Trigger rules don't watch conversations, Ask or Plan. Tool rules don't reach
  Codex, Claude Code, OpenCode or other outside tools, which run their own
  tools, and on every outside tool except Codex a text rule reads the text
  after secrets are removed, so a rule looking for a secret may not fire
  there. A correction asked of a tool other than Codex was not tested, and a
  rule that asks a work loop run to correct still can't reach it: the
  correction is recorded and shown as not sent.

  Helpers that take pieces of a loop run's work, each in its own copy of the
  files, and a lead that runs a small team of them are built but cannot be
  started from the app yet. The Console still has no History screen and no
  Make tasks from this plan.

  Every route except the Nectovia Agent still runs on, and is billed to, your
  own account. A provider connection you saved yourself works only for a
  business whose plan includes the Nectovia Agent, or for Personal work on an
  Individual plan. You can still choose such a connection for a thread, and
  Settings has no place to change or remove it.
