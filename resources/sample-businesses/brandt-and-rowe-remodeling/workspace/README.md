# Brandt & Rowe Remodeling

A residential remodeler in Millbrook: kitchens, baths and the occasional garage conversion. Three jobs are active this week (Henderson, Kessler, Marsh) and two finished over the summer (Alvarez, Okafor).

- `jobs/` the job list, and a folder for each active job with the walkthrough in front of it
- `billing/` each job's draw schedule and every invoice sent to a client, one file each
- `change-orders/` every change order and a register
- `invoices/` supplier invoices, one file each, and a register
- `delivery-tickets/` what was signed for on each supplier delivery
- `subs/` subcontractor bids and invoices
- `permits/` inspections, and the inspection card for the Alvarez final
- `contract/`, `purchase-orders/`, `selections/` the Kessler allowances, purchase orders and selections
- `messages/` texts with clients
- `team.md` people, clients, subs and suppliers
- `owner-notes.md` what the owner keeps meaning to check
