# Juniper Street Bakery & Cafe

A neighborhood bakery and cafe on Juniper Street in Millbrook. Open 6-3 Monday to Saturday, 7-3 Sunday.
Six staff. The point of sale exports a weekly summary every Monday into `exports/`.

- `sales/` weekly item sales, nine weeks (W30 to W38)
- `exports/` the point-of-sale weekly summary
- `suppliers/` Hollis Dairy & Dry Goods price sheets, August and September
- `inventory/` the Sunday close count
- `staff/` the roster and the schedules for last week and this week
- `customers/` feedback log
- `catering/` open inquiries and confirmed orders
- `menu/` the fall menu, with catering tray prices
- `operations/` the owner's open questions
- `briefs/` last week's operations brief
