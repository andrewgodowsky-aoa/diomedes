> FICTIONAL SAMPLE DATA. Larch & Lantern Wine Shop, its distributors, club members and everyone named here are invented for a Nectovia demonstration. Phone numbers use the 555-01xx range and email addresses use example.com.

# Larch & Lantern Wine Shop

A wine shop with a small bar on Alder Street in Millbrook. About 28 wines by the bottle, a Thursday to Saturday bar, and a wine club charged on the third Sunday of the month.

- `catalog/` the wines on the shelf
- `distributors/` posted price lists for Fall Line Wine Co. and Tidewater Imports, and how ordering works
- `sales/` weekly bottle sales for nine weeks (W30 to W38) and last weekend by day
- `bar/` weekly bar sales
- `inventory/` the Sunday close count
- `invoices/` every distributor invoice for the last 90 days, one file each, and a register
- `receiving/` what was counted in against each invoice line
- `credits/` credit memos from the distributors
- `statements/` monthly distributor statements
- `club/` the September club charge run
- `reorder/` last week's approved reorder
- `operations/` the owner's notes
- `staff/` who does what
