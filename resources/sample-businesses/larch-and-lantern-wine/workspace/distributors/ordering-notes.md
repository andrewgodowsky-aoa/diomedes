# How we order

- **Fall Line Wine Co.** delivers Tuesdays. Orders must reach them by **noon Monday**. Rep: Gil Tanaka, 555-0161, orders@fall-line.example.com.
- **Tidewater Imports** delivers every other Thursday. Orders by 3 PM Wednesday. Rep: Rosa Iglesias, 555-0162, orders@tidewater.example.com.
- Both post a price list. Some wines have a case break: order the break quantity or more on one invoice and the whole line is billed at the break price.
- Breakage and corked wine: photo, then ask the rep for a credit memo. The credit should show on the next monthly statement.

## The reorder rule

Reorder any wine with **less than two weeks of stock**, where a week is the average of the last four weeks of sales (W35 to W38 this week). Order enough **whole cases** to bring it to four weeks of stock. The reorder spends money, so Lise approves it before it goes out.
