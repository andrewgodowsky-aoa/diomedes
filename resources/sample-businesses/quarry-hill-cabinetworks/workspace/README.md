# Quarry Hill Cabinetworks

A four-person cabinet and furniture shop in Millbrook. Kitchens, vanities, built-ins and the occasional table. Sheet goods arrive on Tuesdays and are counted Monday and Friday.

- `stock/` the Monday and Friday sheet-goods counts, the issue log and the remnant rack
- `deliveries/` delivery notes as signed for
- `invoices/` supplier invoices
- `jobs/` the job list and a folder per job: cut lists, estimates, punch lists and site notes
- `team.md` people and suppliers
- `owner-notes.md` what the owner wants to know
